Round 39 · Full screen + TradingView-style categories

Drawn at your screen width (1911 px). Part 1: how wide the site should go (all pages). Part 2: Charts home organised like TradingView's Markets page. Part 3: the stock page with TradingView's tabs (Overview, Chart, Financials, Technicals, Forecasts, Seasonals, News, Dates).

1 · Page width (every page)Today the site stops at 1280 px, so on your screen ~350 px each side is empty (striped). Blue labels = measured.
W1 · Wide: up to 1760 px RECOMMENDEDFills a 1920 screen with ~75 px margins. The extra room goes to a 3rd feed column and a wider right column, so lines of text stay easy to read.
W2 · Edge to edgeUses every pixel (24 px margins). Most data on screen; on very wide monitors the layout stretches and text lines get long.
2 · Charts home, TradingView "Markets" style (shown at W1)Category bar on top (jumps down the page). Each category = a row of symbols you click + one big chart of the selected one + its top movers. Your watchlist and important dates stay on the right, sticky.
3 · Stock page with TradingView tabs (shown at W1)Breadcrumb, big header, then tabs. "Overview" (shown) = chart + key facts + previews of the other tabs. "Chart" = the full drawing chart filling the screen. Below: what Technicals, Forecasts, Financials and Seasonals look like.
  • Technicals (Buy / Sell gauges): calculated in your browser from the candles we already load (RSI, MACD, Stochastic, CCI, moving averages 10-200), for 1 hour / 1 day / 1 week. Free, no new data.
  • Forecasts: analysts' price target (low / average / high) and buy-hold-sell counts from Yahoo (robot, once a day).
  • Financials: revenue and net income by quarter and year, margins, dividends, from Yahoo (robot, once a day). Seasonals: average return per month over the past years, calculated from monthly candles.
  • Not copied from TradingView: Community, Options, ETFs, Bonds, Filings tabs (no free data, or not useful for you).
