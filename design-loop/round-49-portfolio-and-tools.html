Round 49

Five new features in the site's current dark graphite look. Stock names and prices are real (from your screens). Your holdings, share counts, costs and P&L are made up for the mockup. Each part has a recommendation. You can pick per part, e.g. "P2, I1, T1, S1+S2, W1".

Part 1 · Portfolio tracker (new page 4: "Portfolio" in the top bar, next to Charts)

Stored in your Supabase database, readable only by your login (the same lock that guards your notes). Never in the public GitHub repo. Prices come from the live price feed you already have. Thai stocks are in THB and US stocks in USD, so totals are converted to THB with today's USD/THB rate (you can switch to USD).

P1 · Simple holdings
You type each stock once: shares + average cost. To change it, you edit the row. Fastest to set up. Tradeoff: no history, so no realised profit (profit you've already taken by selling) and no dividend record. If you buy more, you work out the new average cost yourself.

P2 · Trade ledger (my pick)
You record each Buy / Sell / Dividend (date, shares, price, fee). The page works out average cost, realised P&L (profit locked in by selling), unrealised P&L and dividends received, the same way a broker statement does. More typing per trade. Tradeoff: if you skip a trade, the numbers drift. An "Opening balance" entry lets you start with what you own today, with no history needed.

Part 2 · World impact on your holdings

Mostly exists already: each World story has an AI "Thailand & your stocks" line, written from your watchlist. This change adds your holdings to what the AI sees, and shows how much of your portfolio is touched. No new AI calls: it's the same request, with a longer list.

I1 · Holding badge under the line (only option, small change)
An orange badge appears only when a story names a stock you own (watchlist-only stocks keep today's look). The % is that stock's weight in your portfolio. My News also ranks stories about stocks you own a little higher.

Part 3 · Long / Short position tool (chart drawing toolbar)

Like TradingView's tool: click entry, drag the target and the stop. A green box shows profit and a red box shows risk. It works with your existing drawing features (Object tree, settings window, templates, alerts).

T1 · TradingView-style boxes + settings window (only option)
Labels on the box: target (+% and price), stop (−%), risk : reward ratio. Settings window: account size + risk % → it tells you how many shares to buy. Account size can fill in from your Portfolio value.

Part 4 · Screener filters on the Stocks page

Adds number filters next to your Market / Industry dropdowns. Available for all 1,204 stocks: P/E, forward P/E, dividend yield, market cap, today's change, distance from 52-week high / low. The robot already asks Yahoo for market caps in batches of ~50 stocks, so it can collect these numbers in the same daily request (about 25 calls, no extra cost). Guess to verify while building: Yahoo returns P/E and yield for most US/Japan/Korea/HK/Europe stocks, but some Thai stocks may come back blank. Blanks are hidden while a filter is on.

S1 · "+ Filter" button in the existing bar
Click "+ Filter" and pick a number. Type a range (min / max). It becomes an amber token you can remove with ×. Matching columns are added to the table.
