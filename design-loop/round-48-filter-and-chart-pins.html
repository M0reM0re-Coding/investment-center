Part 2 · News pins (#1) + events (#2) on the stock chart

Both get an on/off switch in the chart's top bar. News = your stories with impact 6+ on this stock (last 90 days: the robot deletes older ones). Events = earnings, dividends (this stock) + Fed / CPI / jobs (US stocks) or BOT (Thai stocks).

My recommendation: F1 (one row, grouped US / Thailand, also fixes the confusing look-alike names like "Food & Beverage" vs "Food & Beverages") + P1 (pins sit where the price reacted, so cause → effect reads at a glance; events stay out of the way on the bottom edge).
