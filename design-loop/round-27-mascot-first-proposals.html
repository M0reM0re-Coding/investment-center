BIG NEWS
How the mood is picked (no extra AI call):
  • Bullish: the story's sentiment is Bullish (e.g. NVDA +30%).
  • Bearish: sentiment Bearish (e.g. worst week since 2020).
  • Breaking: impact 10, or a brand-new story under 1 hour old.
  • Serious: conflict, disaster or deaths (war, strikes, attacks): the mascot never smiles at these.
  • Caught up: empty states, e.g. "All 5 read" on World Brief.
