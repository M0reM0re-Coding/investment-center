Round 40 · Heat map page, full-screen chart, foldable sidebar

Width W1 (1760 px) as you chose, drawn in Dark like your screen. Blue labels explain what each part does; they are not part of the design.

1 · Stock heat map page (Charts → Heat map)Like TradingView: one tile per company, size = company value, colour = today's move, grouped by sector, logos on big tiles. Pick S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 or SET50; size by market cap or volume; colour by 1 day / 1 week / 1 month. Click a tile = its chart. Sidebar folded here.
2 · Stock page: chart fills the screen, TradingView-style toolsThe chart is the page. Key facts, Financials, Technicals, Forecasts, Seasonals, News and Dates are tabs (one click away, not below the chart). Sidebar open: watchlist + important dates. Shown with the Indicators window open (search, favourites ★, categories).
3 · Same page, sidebar folded (ย่อ)Click the icon strip (or press the ‹ arrow) to fold the panel; the chart gets the extra 310 px. Your choice is remembered. Shown: hovering an indicator's name shows 👁 hide · ⚙ settings · ✕ remove (like TradingView); right-click on the chart opens the menu.
  • Chart interaction like TradingView: drag to move, mouse wheel to zoom, drag the price scale to stretch it, double-click an indicator to edit, crosshair with price + time labels, right-click menu, Delete removes the selected drawing, Ctrl+Z undo. Shortcuts: Alt+T trend line, Alt+F Fibonacci, Alt+H horizontal line, Alt+I indicators.
  • Top bar: symbol (click to search), interval (5m … 1M), chart type (candles, hollow candles, bars, line, area), Indicators, undo/redo; right side: screenshot, full screen. Bottom bar: 1D 5D 1M 3M 6M YTD 1Y 5Y All jump buttons, time zone, % / log scale.
  • Heat map data: the robot saves each index's member list with sector and company size once a day (S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 lists from Wikipedia, SET50 fixed list); today's moves come live from the same Yahoo prices we use now (refreshed every minute while the page is open).
