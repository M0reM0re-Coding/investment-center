Part 1. Read first on World Brief: 2 places

A. Same as My News: a "To read" chip opens the Read first queue

A "To read · 5" chip next to the region chips. Tapping it shows the queue on the right (numbered, NEXT UP, progress bar, ✓ on each row) with the rest of the list beside it. Exactly how My News works, so nothing new to learn. The page stays short when you don't need it.

Part 2. Map: colour per continent + all situations together

Land is tinted by continent (soft colours so the lines stay readable). Each situation is a line between the countries, with a label in the middle: "Ukraine → Russia · STRIKE". Attacks get a red arc with a moving missile trail and an impact flash, plus a red ALERT card for attacks in the last 12 hours. Click a label to open the story. With "Reduced motion" on, nothing moves (lines and labels stay).
