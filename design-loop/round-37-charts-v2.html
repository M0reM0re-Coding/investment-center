Round 37 · Charts: market home → stock page

Your layout: a search bar on top, every market plus the big stocks in the middle, your watchlist and the important stock dates (whole market, not just yours) on the right. Click any stock to open its page: a chart with drawing tools (trend line, Fibonacci, Elliott waves), volume and indicators, plus its key stats, its dates and its news.

  • Chart engine: KLineChart Pro, open source (Apache 2.0, free). It draws our price data, so Thai stocks work. The free TradingView chart refused SET:PTT and SET:AOT in my test ("only available on TradingView").
  • Your drawings are saved to your account for each stock and come back next time (the free TradingView chart forgets them when you reload).
  • Tools: trend line, ray, horizontal line, parallel channel; Fibonacci retracement / extension / fan / circle; Elliott 3-, 5- and 8-wave and any-wave; ABCD / XABCD; Gann box; rectangle, arrow, text; magnet; lock / hide / delete all. Indicators: Volume, MA, EMA, Bollinger, MACD, RSI, KDJ, SAR, OBV, and more.
  • "Open in TradingView ↗" button on every stock, for anything heavier (your own TradingView layouts with your drawings).
  • Prices: Yahoo Finance through our database (same as today's prices), daily and hourly candles; Thai stocks may be ~15 minutes late.
