Round 53: the Pro space + Pro's own theme settings

Click PRO (top right) and the site turns into the terminal. It shows only Pro plan modules (F1-F10). My News, World Brief, Charts, Stocks, Portfolio and the calendars stay in normal mode. Click PRO again to go back. Below: the same Pro home in 4 colour schemes, then the Pro Settings panel. Headlines are real (robot, 26 Nov). All other numbers are sample numbers. Panels for modules that aren't built yet say which phase brings them.

1. Amber2. Phosphor3. Arctic4. Paper (light)Pro Settings
1. Amber (classic)round 52 A as picked: black + amber. The default.
2. Phosphorold green-screen terminal: green on near-black. Most "retro computer". Up and down are harder to tell apart (green everywhere).
3. Arcticdeep navy + ice cyan. Cooler and softer on the eyes at night; less "Bloomberg".
4. Paper (light)the same terminal in daylight: warm paper, black mono text, rust accent. Used when Pro is set to Light, or Auto in daytime.
Pro Settingsopens from the ⚙ next to PRO. Same pattern as the normal Settings (Auto / Light / Dark + a style for each), but with Pro's own choices.
