The strip keeps "Next up", and for 2 hours after a High-impact release it adds a "Just out" item first.

Where the "Actual" numbers come from: Forex Factory's free files have no actual values (checked: even yesterday's released events have none). Options:
  • Nasdaq economic calendar (my pick): no sign-up, no key. It had actuals for 41 of 42 events on 24 Nov, and its forecasts match Forex Factory's. It is unofficial (the data behind Nasdaq's calendar page, not a documented API), so it could change. The robot asks politely, ~once every 30 min after a release time, and doesn't disguise itself.
  • FMP free key: an official API, but you sign up for a free key, and I couldn't confirm the free plan includes the economic calendar.
  • Link only: no actuals; each event links to its Forex Factory page.
If Nasdaq has no match for an event (rare ones, speeches), it shows "—" and links to Forex Factory.
